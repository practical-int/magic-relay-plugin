# Magic Relay for Claude

Run your social accounts from Claude. Magic Relay connects Claude to your X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and Threads accounts. This plugin adds skills that teach Claude how to use it well.

## What you get

- **Brand voice:** writes posts and replies from your saved profile and your own recent posts.
- **Publish flow:** checks a post against each platform's rules, saves a draft, gets your OK on the exact text, then publishes or schedules it and confirms it went out.
- **Analytics readout:** tells you how a post or account is doing, using only the numbers each platform gives.
- **Inbox triage:** finds the comments, mentions and direct messages that need you, and drafts answers for your approval.
- **Daily check-in:** a short brief of what's scheduled, what needs you and how recent posts did.
- **Getting started:** shows what's connected and what to try first. Ask how to begin, or type `/magic-relay:start` in Cowork or Claude Code.

## Use it

1. Add the plugin, then connect Magic Relay from the plugin's **Connectors** tab and sign in.
2. Connect your social accounts on [magicrelay.com](https://magicrelay.com) by signing in on each platform.
3. Ask something like "How did my last post do?" or "Draft a post about our new opening hours."

You need a Magic Relay account with an active plan. What Claude can do differs by platform, and Magic Relay says plainly when a platform doesn't allow something. The [setup guide](https://magicrelay.com/docs/claude) has more.

## Data

This plugin contains only instructions and the address of the Magic Relay server, `https://api.magicrelay.com/mcp`. It runs no code on your computer.

When you use it, Claude sends your requests to that server, including post text, media you add, replies and any feedback you choose to send. The server reads and writes your connected social accounts on your behalf. Read how Magic Relay handles your data in the [privacy policy](https://magicrelay.com/privacy), and how to remove it in the [data deletion guide](https://magicrelay.com/data-deletion).
