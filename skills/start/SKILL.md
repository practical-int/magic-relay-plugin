# Getting started

Call `list_accounts` and show the person, in one short list, which social accounts are connected and any that need reconnecting, with the link from `connect_account` for those.

If nothing is connected yet, give them the link from `connect_account` and stop there.

Otherwise, offer these three things to try, one line each, and wait for them to pick:

1. "How are my accounts doing, and how did my latest post do?"
2. "Draft a post for my accounts, in my voice, and show it to me before anything goes out."
3. "Show the comments, mentions and messages that need me, and draft answers."
